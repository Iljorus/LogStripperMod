## A Log Stripper Mod for Minecraft 1.20.1 ##

The LogStripperMod is an independent Minecraft mod that does exactly what the title says: It adds one and only one machine called the "Automatic Log Stripper".


### Why use this mod? ###

We all know that feeling: You just started work on your newest build and realize you need stacks upon stacks of stripped logs :/  
Well fear no more for the **Log Stripper Mod** is here to the rescue!  
Simply place your logs in the machine and take out your freshly stripped ones.


## Config ##

Balancing of various features is done in the *config* file.  
Take a look and see what fits your playthrough best.
